4. Live database guarantees (20 of 20 passing)

  • Direct table reads and writes with the public key are refused (HTTP 401).
  • Internal functions cannot be called from outside.
  • Oversized days (301 slots), over-long or control-character names are refused.
  • The desk refuses a missing or wrong owner key.
  • An offer accepted after its 15-minute hold is refused.
  • A fourth message to one customer in a day is refused.
  • A mistaken cancellation can be undone.
  • Stylist marked out: their open offers revoked, gaps held, a late tap cannot book.
  • An absent stylist's gap cannot be offered.
  • Moves refuse a busy or unskilled stylist.
  • A stylist's key opens only their own day, never the desk.
  • Customer sign-up needs an explicit consent choice; the private token manages only that entry.
  • Front-desk key runs the day but is refused policy changes and link creation.
  • Owner rule changes are bounds-checked.
  • A stylist who has gone home is never offered: no option after their shift.
  • A customer picks a new time from their link: moved exactly once.
  • A customer cancelling from their link opens the slot for the waitlist at once.
  • A burst of 260 writes is cut off at 240 per minute.
  • One confirmed booking per slot, whatever path writes it.
  • Every action lands in an append-only evidence log.

6. Change made after MBA feedback

The business team asked for a way to reschedule instead of only cancelling. Shipped in v2.0.0: the desk can move any booking, or send the client a private link listing only times that truly work (right service, staff member present and on shift, slot free). The time they leave opens for the waitlist. A flaw found while building it was fixed the same evening: staff now have working hours, so nothing is ever moved onto someone who is not at work.
